Agent Chain

Five agents coordinate the placement lifecycle

Scout

Reads resumes, extracts strengths and gaps, and rewrites the placement narrative around evidence.

Matcher

Finds role fit, scores shortlist probability, and explains missing capabilities blocking interviews.

Planner

Turns insight into a concrete multi-week prep plan with role-aware priorities.

Interviewer

Generates mock interview questions, critiques answers, and evolves a coaching report in real time.

Watchdog

Monitors readiness risk and alerts the TPC when intervention is needed before placement windows close.
